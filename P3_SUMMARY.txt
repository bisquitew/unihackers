1. EXECUTIVE OVERVIEW
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
As P3 in the team split, our mission was to build the social, cultural, and 
civic connection layer for Timișoara residents, students, and visitors.

We delivered four complete "vertical slices" (data contracts, seed data, 
business service logic, REST API routes, AI assistant tools, Postgres schema 
migrations, and React Native Expo UI screens):

  1. [M08_FORUM]  — Hyperlocal community board (Lost pets, mutual aid, cleanups)
  2. [M01_EVENTS] — Cultural micro-events calendar & RSVP ticket booking
  3. [M07_BUDDY]  — "Cu Cine?" companion finder for solo attendees & Erasmus
  4. [M09_WALLET] — Solana Devnet ticketing, QR check-in & Proof of Attendance (POAP)

3. WHAT EACH MODULE DOES (DETAILED FUNCTIONALITY)
================================================================================

[M08_FORUM] — COMMUNITY & NEIGHBOURHOOD FORUM
- Problem: Community questions, lost pets, and mutual aid are fragmented across
  unmoderated social media groups with no neighbourhood localization.
- How It Works:
  * Categorization: Posts are organized by 'lost_pets', 'help', 'neighbourhood'.
  * Localization: Every post is tagged with a Timișoara neighbourhood (Fabric,
    Iosefin, Complexul Studențesc, Cetate, etc.).
  * Citizen Engagement: Residents can upvote helpful posts and participate in
    nested discussions.
  * Civic Moderation: Users can report inappropriate or spam posts. Reported
    posts enter an isolated moderation queue (/moderation/reported) for review.
  * AI Integration: Exposes Gemini function tools so the city assistant can
    instantly answer citizen questions like "Are there any lost pets in Fabric?".

[M01_EVENTS] — CULTURAL MICRO-EVENTS CALENDAR
- Problem: Timișoara has major annual festivals but lacks visibility for small
  pop-up culture: indie cinema, acoustic sessions, and creative workshops.
- How It Works:
  * Discovery: Filter micro-events by category (cinema, music, workshop, showcase),
    neighbourhood, or age group.
  * Booking: Tapping "Participă & Emite Bilet" instantly registers the user,
    increments attendee capacity, and mints an entrance ticket on Solana Devnet.
  * Home Widget: Provides EventsHomeCard for P1's main app shell so upcoming
    highlights appear on the home screen automatically.
  * AI Integration: Exposes 'find_local_events' for Gemini conversational queries.

[M07_BUDDY] — "CU CINE?" (EVENT BUDDY FINDER)
- Problem: International Erasmus students, newcomers, and solo residents often
  skip cultural events because they have no one to go with.
- How It Works:
  * Buddy Requests: Attendees can opt in to "Looking for company" on any event.
  * Interest Matching: Matches attendees using interest tags (#film, #techno,
    #pottery, #jazz) and calculates compatibility scores.
  * Icebreakers: Prompts conversation with pre-built discussion questions.
  * Small Group Chats: Creates lightweight group chats for matched attendees.

[M09_WALLET] — SOLANA DEVNET TICKETS & POAP
- Problem: Targets the "Best Use of Solana" sponsor prize (Hardware Wallets).
- How It Works:
  * Zero-Cost Minting: Issues tamper-proof digital tickets on Solana Devnet at
    near-zero transaction cost (< $0.0002).
  * On-Chain Verifiability: Generates standard 88-char base58 transaction
    signatures and links directly to live Solana Explorer (Devnet).
  * Venue Check-In: Constructs a compact JSON QR payload for scanning at the door.
  * POAP: Validates check-in on-chain and issues a Proof-of-Attendance badge.
