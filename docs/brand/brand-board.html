Colour

Pink is rare: the logo and the one main button on a screen. Everything else is white and ink. Blue, mint and apricot appear only inside illustrations, as small dots, on avatars and as status glyphs.

Pink FF3D7F. Logo and main button only. Always an ink label (5.3:1); white would only reach 3.4:1

Blue 3D5BFF solid, 2438C9 deep (8.5:1 on white). Transit, parking, information

Mint 12C99B solid, 00704F deep (6.1:1). Utilities, success, "live"

Apricot FF7A2F solid, A84300 deep (6.1:1). Safety, warnings, avatars

Red B3261E (6.5:1 on white). Errors only

Neutrals F8F6FB board background, white phone surface, 1A1033 ink (16.6:1), 5A5070 muted (6.9:1)

Modules

Small flat illustrations, not pictograms: little scenes built from simple filled shapes in the palette, with no outlines, on a 56 px grid. Bunting for events, a tram with its pantograph, a lamp with its glow, a folded map with a pin. Each stands free on the page with its label beside it. Community modules are neutral; the rest follow blue, mint and apricot. Used for module shortcuts only. Rows, controls and navigation use Phosphor.
